# AnnaSaathi launch film — three storylines

**The one thing:** AnnaSaathi stops food waste *at the shop*. It tells a family how much to buy and what to cook first, from how they actually eat.
**What a viewer remembers:** "Buy 1 kg of tomatoes, not 2. The app worked it out."
**The spine:** the Patil family's real bill line, `TOMATO HYBRID 2.000 KG`, travels from the first frame to the payoff.

Facts used on screen, all from the PRD or the running app (demo household, 31°C):
2 kg bought → 1.25 kg projected to spoil; family of 5 eats like 4.1 adults; tomatoes keep 4–6 days, 3–5 at 31°C;
buy 1 kg, top up 500 g on Wed; palak paneer rescues ₹127; 18 bill lines read; UNEP: 55 kg per person per year at home;
simulator: 50 homes × 90 days, waste −31% / −44% / −64% at 50 / 70 / 100% advice-following, run-outs 62 → 43 a month.

---

## A · "Anna wakes up" (recommended: the robot carries a story a stranger follows)

- **Device / form:** a character does the work (the orange robot is AnnaSaathi's kitchen agent) · an object-to-object chain.
- **What you see and feel:** a sleeping robot on a kitchen counter wakes when the week's bill lands, and spots 1.25 kg of tomatoes that will rot. Every feature after that is the robot acting on the same bill, so each cut is caused by the last. You come away feeling a helper has the week under control.
- **First 2 s:** the real kirana receipt, big and tilted, with `TOMATO HYBRID 2.000 KG` swiped in haldi. The robot is asleep beside it, Zzz on its face. Title: "2 kg of tomatoes."
- **Beats:** hook (bill lands, "1.25 kg will rot") → tension (55 kg a year; expiry apps warn too late) → reveal (haldi flood, AnnaSaathi) → proof (tell · scan · know · cook · buy · check) → payoff (teal flood, −31 → −44 → −64%) → claim ("decided at the shop, not the bin") → end card.
- **Look:** the app's own world: tamarind #1a1411 kitchen at dusk under one warm haldi key light, haldi and teal colour floods, Fraunces display with Bricolage text, the real UI at 2×.
- **Sound:** no music bed (none available without a HeyGen account); exact bundled effects on visible actions: pop on wake, click on taps, whoosh on whips, riser into the −64% payoff, bass impact on the mark.

## B · "A tomato's week"

- **Device / form:** life before and after · one continuous take along a Mon → Sun shelf.
- **What you see and feel:** one tomato ages across a week on a 31°C counter, browning, until it falls into the bin. Rewind: the same week with AnnaSaathi, where 1 kg arrives, a top-up lands Wednesday and nothing reaches the bin. Calm, clear cause and effect.
- **First 2 s:** a single red tomato on a counter, the day label "Sun · bought 2 kg" ticking to "Mon".
- **Beats:** buy too much → it ages → bin → rewind → buy right → cook first → nothing wasted → number → end card.
- **Look:** warm terracotta counter, a day ruler as the only graphic, macro produce photography (would need real photos).
- **Sound:** clock ticks per day, a soft thud in the bin, a rewind whoosh.

## C · "55 kg" (the bold one)

- **Device / form:** a number lands · cut on the beat, giant kinetic type.
- **What you see and feel:** "55 kg" fills the frame as a physical pile. Each feature knocks a slab off the pile until −44% is left standing. Loud, fast, poster-like.
- **First 2 s:** "55" slams full-bleed in haldi on tamarind, then "kg of food per person, binned at home, every year".
- **Beats:** number → why (bought too much) → five feature hits, each removing a slab → what's left → end card.
- **Look:** two-colour poster, haldi on tamarind, giant Fraunces numerals, hard cuts.
- **Sound:** needs a strong music bed to carry the cuts (not available offline), which is why it isn't the pick.
